Questions

Things people ask me

Do I own the website?
Yes. It's yours — the design, the code and the domain. There's no monthly fee to me and you can move it to another developer any time. I don't hold your site hostage.
How long does it take?
Most single-page sites are live in 3 days. Sites with product catalogues or listings take about 5. That's assuming I have your photos, prices and details. If those arrive late, the timeline moves with them.
Can I update it myself afterwards?
Yes, and I show you how before we finish. If you sell products or rent out property, you'll be able to add and change listings yourself. If you'd rather I do it, I can — but you won't need me for small changes.
What do you need from me?
Your business name, logo if you have one, your phone number and location, opening hours, and photos of your work. A WhatsApp voice note answering those questions is enough to get started.
How do I pay?
MTN MoMo, Airtel Money or bank transfer. 50% up front so I know you're serious, 50% when the site is live and you're happy. Receipt for every payment.
What if I don't like it?
You see the finished site before the final payment, and one round of changes is included in every plan. If it's still not right, tell me — I'd rather fix it than lose the job and the reputation.
Do I need a domain?
If you don't have one, I'll set one up for you. It costs roughly UGX 40,000 per year, registered in your name. You can use it forever — it doesn't expire with me.
